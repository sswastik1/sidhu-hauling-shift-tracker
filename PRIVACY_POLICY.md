# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 28, 2026**

Sidhu Hauling Ltd. ShiftTracker is an internal workforce-management app operated by Swastik Sharma for Sidhu Hauling Ltd. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

## Information we collect

Depending on how the app is used, we may collect:

- Account information, such as an email address and authentication details, when an employee signs in or creates an account.
- Work records, such as clock-in and clock-out times, shift notes, fuel information, ticket information, and generated reports.
- Device location when an employee grants location permission and uses location-related features. Location is used to record work locations and display authorized live driver locations.
- Images selected by an employee, such as ticket or fuel-receipt images attached to a shift record.
- Technical information required by Firebase and Apple to provide authentication, storage, security, and app functionality.

## How we use information

We use information to:

- Authenticate employees and manage access to the app.
- Record and manage work hours and shift details.
- Support driver scheduling, location visibility, and operational reporting.
- Store and display images attached to work records.
- Maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the app.

## Sharing and service providers

This app is intended for Sidhu Hauling Ltd. personnel. Information is shared with authorized company users who need it for workforce and operations management. The app uses Google Firebase services for authentication, database storage, and file storage. Firebase processes information on our behalf according to its applicable terms and privacy practices. We do not sell personal information.

## Location permissions

Location access is requested only for location-related app features. You can manage location permissions in your device settings. Turning off permission may prevent location records or live-location features from working.

## Data retention and security

We retain information for as long as it is needed for business, accounting, operational, legal, or security purposes. We use access controls and the security features provided by Firebase and Apple, but no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

## Your choices and requests

You may manage device permissions through iOS Settings. Employees may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their account or associated information by contacting the app administrator.

## Children's privacy

The app is intended for employees and business users. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when changes are published.
