# Sidhu Hauling Ltd. ShiftTracker Support

Sidhu Hauling Ltd. ShiftTracker is an employee shift and driver-management app for authorized Sidhu Hauling Ltd. personnel.

## Getting help

For support, contact:

- **Email:** [swazz1069@gmail.com](mailto:swazz1069@gmail.com)
- **Company:** Sidhu Hauling Ltd.

When reporting a problem, include the iPhone or iPad model, iOS/iPadOS version, app version, and the steps that caused the problem. Do not include passwords, Firebase credentials, or private employee information.

## Common issues

### Login or registration

Confirm that the email and password are correct. New employees must enter the company access code supplied by Sidhu Hauling Ltd.

### Location features

Allow location access when prompted. Location permission can also be changed under **Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services**.

### Shift records or uploads

Use a reliable internet connection and keep the app open until clock-out finishes. Ticket and fuel-receipt images are uploaded when the shift is saved.

### Account deletion

Signed-in employees can select **Delete Account** on the Home screen, confirm the warning, enter their current password, and select **Delete My Account**.
